# Graphiant

> Graphiant is a value-driven Network-as-a-Service platform for secure AI, cloud, and distributed environments.

Graphiant helps enterprises, service providers, and public-sector organizations replace fragmented networking and security tools with one unified, AI-powered platform. The service delivers connectivity, security, visibility, policy control, and AI-driven operations through a single subscription.

Graphiant connects branches, data centers, public clouds, neo-clouds, remote users, AI agents, applications, customers, suppliers, and business partners. Its cloud-native architecture is designed to reduce infrastructure cost and complexity while providing private connectivity, end-to-end encryption, real-time visibility, policy enforcement, data sovereignty controls, and scalable performance.

## Use Cases

- [Multi-Cloud Networking](https://www.graphiant.com/use-cases/cloud-networking): Managed, usage-based connectivity across AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, Oracle Cloud, data centers, and edge locations.
- [Data Loss Prevention](https://www.graphiant.com/use-cases/dlp): Policy-based protection for sensitive, regulated, and proprietary information, including inspection without decrypting traffic in the network core.
- [Data Sovereignty](https://www.graphiant.com/use-cases/data-sovereignty): Geographic route enforcement, end-to-end encryption, stateless forwarding, and real-time verification designed to keep data within approved regions.
- [Financial Payments Network](https://www.graphiant.com/use-cases/payments): Secure, policy-driven connectivity for payment ecosystems, financial institutions, merchants, applications, clouds, and business partners.
- [Gina AI](https://www.graphiant.com/use-cases/gina-ai): Graphiant's AI assistant for network operations, visibility, policy guidance, reporting, troubleshooting, and actionable insights.
- [Neo-Cloud Connectivity](https://www.graphiant.com/use-cases/neo-cloud): Connectivity between enterprise data, public clouds, AI clouds, GPU infrastructure, and neo-cloud providers.
- [Zero Trust Network Access](https://www.graphiant.com/use-cases/ztna): Identity- and policy-based access for users, AI agents, applications, and private enterprise resources.
